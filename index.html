Mobile controls

CarveHold anywhere and drag your thumb left or right. Further is sharper.
JumpHold JUMP to crouch, release at the lip to pop. Longer hold, higher pop.
SpinIn the air, circle a finger fast. Faster circles spin faster, either direction, up to 2160.
FlipIn the air, swipe up for a front flip or down for a back flip.
Boost and brakeHold BOOST to speed up or BRAKE to slow down. You never stop completely.
AdrenalineWhen the meter is full, the circle under it glows red and pulses. Tap it.

Select a key to change it, then press the new key. Esc cancels.
